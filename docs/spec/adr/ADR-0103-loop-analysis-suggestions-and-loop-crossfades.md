# ADR-0103 — Loop Analysis Measures The Seam Canonically And Suggests Ranked Candidates, A Loop's Crossfade Is Part Of Its Region's Sound, And Only The Person Moves A Loop

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Decision:** `REQ-GAME-121` is `packages/loop-analysis`, run by the detection worker over a region's processed audio, with the loop's crossfade realised in the region's plan.
  - **The loop is the region's.** The loop being analysed is `Region.loop` (`AnchoredLoop`, Phase 05), placed as the domain's `LoopDefinition`; the packet's contract names them, and no second loop value is made. A region holds at most one loop, which export writes as one forward endless loop (`ADR-0093`).
  - **The metrics.** For a loop from frame S up to E of a region, over each channel, with W the analysis window (50 ms, between 256 and 8,192 frames and at most a quarter of the loop): the step across the seam against the windows' first-difference RMS; the click score, the seam's second difference over the windows' second-difference RMS; the DC mismatch between the means of the last and first W frames, and the loop's net DC; the level mismatch between their RMS in dB; the phase mismatch at the dominant partial, where one stands 20 dB above the median of the spectrum; the spectral mismatch, the log-spectral distance over third-octave bands of the canonical STFT; the distance of each boundary from the nearest zero crossing, by the snapping definition now stated once in `packages/audio-engine`; the crossfade's suitability, from the correlation of the faded audio with the audio it replaces and the level change each fade shape gives; and the cycle's stability, the seam's 10 ms envelope against the loop's own by median absolute deviation. Each has a unit, a value, the worst channel and a verdict, pass, warn, fail or not applicable with the reason, against thresholds the plan's table states. Every value is computed with the basic operations and the canonical primitives of `ADR-0032` in a stated order, so an analysis is the same on every machine and a golden test pins it.
  - **Candidates.** Asked to search, the analysis looks within a radius of each boundary (20 ms by default, at most 2 s), keeping the loop's length where asked: boundary pairs are ranked first by the similarity of the audio around the end to the audio around the start, by FFT cross-correlation of each start's neighbourhood over the end's window, then the best 512 pairs are measured in full and ordered by one cost over the metrics and the distance moved. Ties fall to the smaller move, then the earlier start, then the earlier end, so the same audio, settings and algorithm version give the same list. A candidate whose seam does not pass is offered with the shortest micro-crossfade (2, 5, 10, 20 or 50 ms, within the audio before the start) and the fade shape whose seam would pass, measured on the crossfaded seam.
  - **Only the person moves a loop.** The analysis returns a value. Applying a candidate or a crossfade is the project's region command, one step the person asks for, which undoes; a failed, cancelled or refused analysis changes nothing, and a result is never applied because it arrived (the packet's invariant and failure rule). A result is kept for its session against the fingerprint of the plan it measured, and is shown as stale once the region's sound changes.
  - **The crossfade is part of the region's sound.** A loop gains its crossfade's shape, `crossfadeShape`, one of the domain's fade shapes, beside its `crossfadeLength` C, so the shape the analysis suggests is the shape written and heard. The region's plan realises both: the last C frames of the loop become the loop's end faded out summed with the C frames before its start faded in, by the domain's mix source as a punch's boundary is (`ADR-0072`), after the region's rack. Playback, export, the peaks and analysis therefore hear what a game engine plays when it jumps from E to S, and a file's loop points are exact without a crossfade the format cannot state. A loop's crossfade needs C frames of the region before S; a longer one is refused by the domain's region validation. Analysing for candidates reads the region without its loop's crossfade, since the crossfade belongs to the points being moved.
  - **Audition by cycling.** Repeated-cycle audition and the seam stress test are plans of segments of the region's stream: cycles of S to E after a lead-in, or the half-second either side of the seam, repeated; played as a programme, extended by the continued programme of `ADR-0104` before it runs out, so cycling is gapless and lasts until stopped, and either is rendered offline the same way for a test.
- **Drivers:** `REQ-GAME-121` (boundary, DC, level, phase, click, zero-crossing, spectral, crossfade and repeated-cycle checks; candidate boundaries and micro-crossfades under the person's control; repeated cycling and stress-testing; loop semantics preserved in export), `REQ-GAME-023` (seamless-loop creation, loop-point editing, zero-crossing assistance, loop auditioning), the packet's acceptance criterion of reproducible suggestions on known discontinuity fixtures, `ADR-0032`, `ADR-0051`. No transport loops today, the loop's crossfade was recorded and never heard, and an export writes loop points alone, so a crossfade only the editor applied would be lost in every file.
- **Constraints:** analysis reads bounded windows and one streamed pass over the loop, never the whole region at once (G4); it runs in the detection worker with the reference DSP, takes a cancellation signal and reports progress. A change to a metric, threshold or the search is a change of the analysis's version and of its golden values (`REQ-EXEC-180`).
- **Change record:** affected requirements `REQ-GAME-121`, `REQ-GAME-023`, `REQ-EDIT-014` (loop settings), `REQ-AUDIO-010` (loop metadata); affected phases 05 (whose loop's crossfade becomes audible, `ADR-0051` amended), 09 (whose export writes the crossfaded audio under unchanged loop points) and 11 (whose loop metadata handling reads the same loops). Compatibility impact: a loop gains its crossfade's shape, its crossfade is now heard, and one longer than the audio before its start is refused, within the document raise of `ADR-0106`. Public API: `LoopAnalysis`, `LoopMetrics`, `LoopCandidate`, `LoopAnalysisRequest`, `analyseLoop`, the audition plans in `@audiogubbins/loop-analysis`; the loop request of the detection worker; the zero-crossing boundary in `@audiogubbins/audio-engine`. Godot interchange and runtime: the exported loop points and crossfaded audio are what an engine loops. PWA and browser: none. Verification: `pnpm test:loop-golden` over the discontinuity fixtures; property tests of the crossfade in the plan against the domain's oracle; a failed and a cancelled analysis leaving the loop unchanged.
- **Amends:** `ADR-0051`, whose region's plan realises its loop's crossfade after its rack; `ADR-0061`, whose detection worker runs the loop analysis. Their other clauses stand.
- **Related requirements:** `REQ-GAME-121`, `REQ-GAME-023`, `REQ-EDIT-013`, `REQ-EDIT-014`, `REQ-AUDIO-010`, `REQ-ARCH-049`, `REQ-ARCH-081`.
