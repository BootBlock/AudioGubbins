Spleeter, two stems, a model pack for AudioGubbins

model.onnx holds the weights of Spleeter's two-stem model (vocals and
accompaniment), from 2stems.tar.gz of the Spleeter v1.4.0 release,
https://github.com/deezer/spleeter/releases/tag/v1.4.0.

Spleeter is Copyright (c) 2019-present, Deezer SA, and licensed under the MIT
License (LICENSE). Its authors state that its source code and pre-trained
models are distributed under the MIT License (Hennequin et al., "Spleeter: a
fast and efficient music source separation tool with pre-trained models",
Journal of Open Source Software 5(50), 2154, 2020, page 3).

AudioGubbins converted it to ONNX (opset 17) with
tools/model-packs/export/spleeter.py (Apache-2.0). The conversion changed the
weights' format and laid out both stems' networks in one graph; it changed no
weight.

The pack was assembled by the AudioGubbins pack build,
tools/model-packs/build-packs.mjs (Apache-2.0).
