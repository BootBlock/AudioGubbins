# AudioGubbins

AudioGubbins is an audio editor for Windows and the web. It keeps your projects on your own
machine, and it never changes your original recordings: every cut, fade and effect is an edit you
can undo, tweak or take back later.

It's built for anyone who needs to clean up and prepare sound, with extra help for people making
games, and it works with Godot 4 projects out of the box.

> **AudioGubbins isn't out yet.** There's nothing to download for now. When the first version is
> ready, the Windows installer will appear on this repository's
> [Releases](https://github.com/BootBlock/AudioGubbins/releases) page.

## What it does

- **Edit without losing anything.** Trim, split, copy, move and fade audio, place markers, and undo
  and redo freely. It's all still there the next time you open the project, and your source files
  are never overwritten.
- **Work offline, on your own machine.** Projects are kept on your computer, not on a server. The
  app works without an internet connection, collects no usage data and sends nothing anywhere
  unless you ask it to.
- **Effects you can change your mind about.** Build chains of effects, reorder or bypass them,
  compare before and after, and save your favourites as presets.
- **Clean up recordings.** Remove noise, hum and clicks, and use optional add-on models that run on
  your machine to restore speech or separate parts of a mix.
- **See and fix sound by frequency.** The spectrogram shows what you hear, and you can select a
  patch of it to quieten, remove or repair a sound.
- **Record.** Choose an input, watch the meters, punch in over a section, keep several takes and
  pick the best. If something crashes mid-take, the recording is still there when you come back.
- **Import and export the usual formats.** WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, Ogg Vorbis and Opus, with presets
  for common jobs and every setting there if you want it.
- **Made for game audio.** Make seamless loops and check them, organise sounds into groups,
  preview how they'll sound in game, and generate sets of variations from one sound.
- **Godot integration.** Export straight into a Godot project, and use the AudioGubbins add-on to
  play your sounds and their variations in your game.
- **On Windows or in your browser.** Install the Windows app, or use it in a web browser. The same
  project opens in either.

## Reporting a problem or an idea

- **Something wrong?** [Report a bug](https://github.com/BootBlock/AudioGubbins/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
  If AudioGubbins lost or damaged your work, please say so in the report, as those come first.
- **An idea?** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/BootBlock/AudioGubbins/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A question, or just want to chat?** Head over to
  [Discussions](https://github.com/BootBlock/AudioGubbins/discussions).

Everything posted here is public. Before you attach a log or a screenshot, please remove anything
personal, such as your name in a folder path (`C:\Users\yourname\...`).

## Security

If you've found a security problem, please don't open an issue. Report it privately instead, as
explained in the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
